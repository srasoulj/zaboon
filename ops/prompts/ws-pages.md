DELIVER

3. Settings `/settings`: daily goal, transliteration and vowel marks (auto/on/off), sound, motion; the keyboard layout only when `meta.flags.persianKeyboard`. BEFORE any identity switch (link, sign-in-then-merge, sign-out) flush the lesson outbox (`(await sharedLessonStores(api)).outbox.flush()`) so the guest's pending writes land first; if the flush can't deliver everything (offline or failing), block the switch with a clear message ("Connect to the internet to save your progress first") instead of stranding the guest's lessons. After a successful merge, re-tag any entries still queued for the guest with `retagUser(guestId, memberId)` once ws-player's outbox exports it.

- Pages under `(app)` must NOT call `useEnsureGuest` (the shell does; a second call creates a second guest). Gate queries with `useSession().status === 'signed_in'`.

- Don't touch `apps/web/public/sounds/**` (ws-player) or `apps/web/public/content/**` (generated, gitignored).
- Persian text: whole words, `lang="fa" dir="rtl"`, ZWNJ intact.
