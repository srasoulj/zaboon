RULES AND PITFALLS
- Pages must NOT call `useEnsureGuest` (the shell does; a second call creates a second guest). Gate queries with `useSession().status === 'signed_in'`; 401s are not retried.
- Keep what the existing specs rely on: for a brand-new guest, `/learn` still ends at `/onboarding` (the shell redirects; don't fight it); `/learn` adds no query params on load (the lesson exit URL must match `/(learn|onboarding)$`); don't add another landmark named "Main"; the `stats`/`daily-goal` test ids belong to the shell.
- Level `kind` is a free string; map it, never crash on an unknown one.
- The active course is the most recently used enrollment. In e2e, make `fixture` active first (e.g. `POST /api/sessions` with `courseId: 'fixture'`) and never assert on fa-en course text (it is AI-drafted and changes).
- Persian text: whole words in their own `FaText`, `lang="fa" dir="rtl"`, ZWNJ intact. Look and feel: the Duolingo-style path from DESIGN-SYSTEM with Zaboon's palette and characters — never Duolingo's names, assets or copy.
