# Hello, tea and first letters

This is the frozen guidebook of the fixture course. It is test data, not course content.

## Saying hello

<fa audio="audio/s_u01_0001.mp3">سلام، خوبی؟</fa> _salām, khubi?_ means "Hello, how are you?".
A friendly reply is <fa audio="audio/s_u01_0008.mp3">خوبم، مرسی</fa> _khubam, mersi_: "I'm good, thanks".

## Spoken first

You learn the spoken form first. When the written form differs, it appears in a small chip.

- نون _nun_ is written نان _nān_: "bread".
- می‌خوام _mikhām_ is written می‌خواهم _mikhāham_: "I want".

## The half-space

می‌خوام is one word: the prefix می joins the verb with a half-space (ZWNJ), not a full space.
That is why it stays a single tile in the word bank.
